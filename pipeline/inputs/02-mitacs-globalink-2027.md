Globalink Research Internship — Mitacs (Canada)

A 12-week initiative that connects post-secondary students, postdoctoral fellows, and academic
researchers from eligible international institutions with Canadian faculty.

Eligibility — students must be:
- at least 18 years of age at the time of application
- enrolled in a full-time undergraduate or combined undergraduate/master's program at an eligible
  institution
- have one to three semesters remaining in their program as of fall 2027
- meet grade requirements for their country
- apply to a minimum of three and a maximum of ten projects, from at least three different Canadian
  provinces
- fluent in the oral and written language of the project (English and/or French)
- available to complete an internship lasting 12 consecutive weeks between May 1 and October 31
- not previously awarded a Globalink Research Internship

Required documents:
- a reference letter from a professor
- a CV (template available)
- academic transcripts (in either English or French)
- language proficiency test results (where required by country)

Eligible countries include: Austria, Brazil, Chile, China, Colombia, Finland, France, Germany,
Hong Kong, India, Jordan, Mexico, **Pakistan**, Peru, Singapore, South Korea, Taiwan, Thailand,
Tunisia, Ukraine, United Kingdom, United States.

Deadlines: Faculty call June 10, 2026 at 1:00 P.M. PT. Student call opens in summer (specific date
not listed on the page).

Disciplines: science, engineering and mathematics through to humanities and social sciences.
