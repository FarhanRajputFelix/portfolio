SPAR — Research Program for AI Risks

A part-time, remote research fellowship that enables aspiring AI safety and policy researchers to
work on impactful research projects with professionals in the field.

Mentees are paired with expert researchers for 3 months of structured research, culminating in a
Demo Day where participants present their findings.

Who it is for: individuals interested in AI safety, policy, security, interpretability, biosecurity,
and the societal impacts of AI.

Eligibility: The program is open to undergraduate, graduate/PhD students, and professionals of
different experience levels. Mentors typically seek applicants with technical backgrounds (ML, CS,
math, physics, biology) or policy expertise (law, public policy, economics). We don't require
previous research experience, and many mentees have none. Applicants who don't feel like a perfect
fit are still encouraged to apply.

Time commitment: Commit 5–40 hours/week, depending on your availability. Typical expectation is
5–20 hours weekly.

What mentees do: conduct research with their team, receive structured support and feedback, present
work at Demo Day, and often continue collaborating after the program toward publications and career
opportunities.

Dates:
- Mentee applications: July 28 – August 18, 2026
- Decisions released: September 4, 2026
- Research period: September 14 – December 14, 2026
- Demo Day: December 19, 2026
