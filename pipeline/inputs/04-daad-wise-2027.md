DAAD WISE — Working Internships in Science and Engineering, 2027 cycle

The German Academic Exchange Service's fully funded summer research internship for undergraduate
students in science, engineering and mathematics.

Duration: 2 to 3 months between May and July at a publicly-funded German university or research
institute, working under a German supervisor.

Funding: monthly scholarship of EUR 831 (calculated at EUR 25 per day), a one-time travel allowance
of EUR 1,050, and DAAD health, accident and personal liability insurance for the internship period.

Eligibility:
- Applicants must be Indian citizens only
- Enrolled in a 4-year undergraduate degree or a 5-year integrated master's degree
- Should be in the 5th or 6th semester of a bachelor's degree
- Must study at a selected DAAD WISE partner institution (IITs, NITs, IISERs, IIITs and selected
  others) in science, engineering or mathematics
- Must not have received a WISE scholarship before

Deadline: 1 November 2026, for internships running May, June or July 2027.
Results announced early February.
