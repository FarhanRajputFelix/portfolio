Visiting Student Research Program (VSRP) — King Abdullah University of Science and Technology

The Visiting Student Research Program (VSRP) at KAUST offers internships for 3rd and 4th-year
bachelor's and master's students in science, technology, engineering, and mathematics (STEM).

Duration: 2–6 months, with flexibility to choose start and end dates year-round.

Eligibility:
- Undergraduate students in 3rd year or above, and master's students
- Minimum GPA: 3.5/4
- English language proficiency (official test scores may be required upon evaluation)
- Valid passport with at least 6 months validity
- Current PhD candidates, KAUST students and KAUST alumni are ineligible

Required documents:
- Official transcript(s) in English for all degrees
- Letter of recommendation from a faculty member
- Statement of purpose
- Curriculum vitae (CV)
- Valid passport

Benefits: USD $1,000 monthly stipend, private bedroom/bathroom, airfare to KAUST and back, access to
core laboratories and research facilities.

Deadline: applications accepted all year round.

Over 100 faculty-led projects available; students choose research topics aligned with their
interests.
