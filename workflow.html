Automation workflow v2 · FL-04 · Week 4

Opportunity → claim-checked outreach pack

A five-step no-code pipeline that turns a job, internship or scholarship posting into a tailored email, CV tweaks and a fit assessment — with a built-in audit step that refuses to let any claim through unless my own knowledge base supports it.

00

Why this pipeline, from my audit

The task I repeat most is the same every time: read a posting, work out whether I actually fit, then write a short email that proves it without overselling. I am applying to internships, research groups and funded graduate programmes, so I do this several times a week and it takes me 25–35 minutes each because I keep re-reading my own CV to remember specifics.

It is also the task where I am most tempted to overclaim. That is why step 4 exists: an adversarial audit that traces every sentence back to evidence and deletes anything it can't support. The pipeline is designed to make me more honest under time pressure, not less.

01

The flow — five steps, named handoffs

1
Claude Project · gather

Extract the posting into a structured requirement list

Paste the raw posting. Output is a numbered list of requirements, each tagged must-have or nice-to-have, plus deadline, contact name and the three keywords the posting repeats.

Handoff: REQUIREMENT LIST (numbered, tagged)
2
Claude Project · synthesize

Match each requirement to real evidence, and mark the gaps

Reads my knowledge files (PROJECT-BRIEF.md, CV facts). Every requirement gets SUPPORTED + the specific evidence, PARTIAL + what's thin, or NO EVIDENCE. Ends with a fit score and an apply / don't-apply call.

Handoff: EVIDENCE MAP + fit score
3
Claude Project · draft

Draft a 150-word email and three CV tweaks

May only use rows marked SUPPORTED or PARTIAL. Anything marked NO EVIDENCE is forbidden, not softened. Output: subject line, email body, three specific CV edits.

Handoff: DRAFT PACK
4
Fresh chat · review

Adversarial audit — trace every claim or cut it

Deliberately run in a new conversation with only the evidence map and the draft, so it can't be persuaded by the earlier reasoning. It labels each sentence TRACED / WEAK / UNSUPPORTED and rewrites the offenders.

Handoff: AUDITED PACK + verdict
5
Claude Project · format

Assemble the send-ready pack

Fixed template every time: subject, email, CV tweaks, deadline, fit score, and the one thing I must verify by hand before sending.

02

The build — every prompt, verbatim

Project setup (one time)

A Claude Project with two knowledge files: PROJECT-BRIEF.md (identity, projects, claim, gather list) and cv-facts.md (dates, grades, credential IDs). Project instructions:

You are my application assistant. You work only from the knowledge files in this
Project: my project brief and my CV facts.

Hard rules:
1. Never state a skill, result, date or credential that is not in the knowledge files.
2. If something is missing, output NO EVIDENCE. Do not soften it, do not infer it,
   do not substitute a similar-sounding fact.
3. Numbers are quoted exactly as written in the files (e.g. R^2 = 0.97, 17
   certifications). Never round, never upgrade.
4. I am a CS undergraduate with no publications. Never describe me as a researcher,
   engineer with industry years, or expert.
5. Prefer specifics over adjectives. "Built a Flutter + FastAPI platform" beats
   "passionate about building".

Output format: whatever the step asks for, nothing else. No preamble.

Step 1 — GATHER

STEP 1 of 5 — GATHER.
Here is a posting. Extract, in this exact format:

DEADLINE: (date or "not stated")
CONTACT: (name/email or "not stated")
REPEATED KEYWORDS: (the 3 phrases the posting uses most)
REQUIREMENTS:
1. [must-have|nice-to-have] requirement, in the posting's own words
2. ...

Do not comment on my fit yet. Do not invent requirements that are not written.

POSTING:
<<paste raw posting>>

Step 2 — SYNTHESIZE

STEP 2 of 5 — EVIDENCE MAP.
Using ONLY the knowledge files, map each requirement from the list above.

For each, output:
N. [SUPPORTED|PARTIAL|NO EVIDENCE] — requirement
   evidence: the specific project, credential or number from my files
   (for PARTIAL, name exactly what is thin)

Then:
FIT SCORE: x/10, counting must-haves only
CALL: apply / apply with caveat / skip — and one sentence why
BIGGEST GAP: the single thing that would most improve my case

Step 3 — DRAFT

STEP 3 of 5 — DRAFT.
Write, using only SUPPORTED and PARTIAL rows:

SUBJECT: (under 60 characters, specific, no buzzwords)
EMAIL: 150 words max. Structure: why them (1 sentence), my strongest matching
evidence with the number attached (2 sentences), one honest gap and how I am
closing it (1 sentence), a clear ask (1 sentence). No "I am passionate about".
CV TWEAKS: exactly 3, each naming the section and the new wording.

Anything marked NO EVIDENCE is forbidden. Do not hint at it.

Step 4 — REVIEW (new chat, no Project context)

You are a skeptical hiring reviewer. Below is an EVIDENCE MAP and a DRAFT EMAIL.
Your job is to catch overclaiming.

For every sentence of the email, output:
[TRACED] sentence — which evidence row supports it
[WEAK] sentence — supported but overstated; give a tighter rewrite
[UNSUPPORTED] sentence — no evidence row; delete it

Then output a CLEAN VERSION with all WEAK lines rewritten and all UNSUPPORTED
lines removed. Finally: SEND / DO NOT SEND, and one sentence why.

EVIDENCE MAP: <<paste>>
DRAFT: <<paste>>

Step 5 — FORMAT

STEP 5 of 5 — PACK.
Assemble the audited email into this exact block:

--- OUTREACH PACK ---
ORG / PROGRAMME:
DEADLINE:
FIT SCORE:      /10
SUBJECT:
EMAIL:
CV TWEAKS: 1. 2. 3.
VERIFY BY HAND: (the one fact I must confirm before sending)
--- END ---

05

Where it breaks, and what a human must still check

Step 2 substitutes a near-neighbour factHigh

Asked about "experience with PyTorch" it may reach for TensorFlow and call it SUPPORTED, because they occupy the same slot in its head. The instruction forbids it, but adjacent facts are the most likely leak. Human check: read every SUPPORTED row against the actual requirement word, not the topic.

Step 4 is only honest in a fresh chatHigh

Run in the same conversation, the auditor has already seen the reasoning that produced the draft and rubber-stamps it. The isolation is the mechanism, not a formality. Human check: confirm it is a new chat with no Project knowledge attached.

Numbers drift when re-typedMedium

R² ≈ 0.97 can become "97% accuracy", which is a different claim. Deadlines can shift by a day. Human check: every number in the final email compared character by character against the source file — this is what the VERIFY BY HAND line is for.

Postings pasted as prose lose structureMedium

Copying from a PDF or a JS-rendered page mangles bullet lists, so step 1 misses requirements entirely. Human check: count the requirements it found against the posting.

Knowledge files go staleMedium

New certificate, new project, new grade — none of it exists to the pipeline until the file is updated, and the model will confidently report NO EVIDENCE for something true. Human check: update the knowledge files before a batch of applications.

Pre-send checklist (never skipped)

  • Every number in the email matches the source file exactly
  • The recipient's name and organisation are spelled correctly — the pipeline never verifies these
  • Deadline confirmed on the original posting, not from the extraction
  • No sentence claims a title I don't hold (researcher, engineer, expert)
  • The stated gap is one I am genuinely closing, and the way I describe closing it is true
